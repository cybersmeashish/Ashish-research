# Connected security research agenda

## Vision

Make complex security decisions inspectable across enterprise transformation, applications, cloud, AI systems, and the post-quantum transition. Publish work that practitioners can reproduce and leaders can use to ask better questions.

## Publication criteria

Publish the question, primary sources, method, baseline, environment, observed result, limitations, and open question. Link code and synthetic fixtures where possible. Credit upstream work and separate an authored artifact from peer review, a submission from acceptance, and a prototype from production use.

## Research progression

Begin with one business process and a bounded question. Add a minimal control, a negative case, benign controls, and a result note. Ask another practitioner to reproduce it. Revise the artifact before expanding the scope.

The long-term ambition is influential security leadership grounded in useful frameworks, original research, and maintained tools. Recognition is an outcome to earn through the work.
